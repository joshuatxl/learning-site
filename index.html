Today in AI

The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence is straining global energy infrastructure while intensifying debates over safety governance. With U.S. data center power demands projected to eclipse the natural gas usage of major nations, industry leaders remain divided on regulation. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang argued against strict safety mandates, even as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind conduct collaborative safety talks in Washington.

Meanwhile, major tech companies are accelerating product releases. Google introduced its Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking dialogue models, while Meta launched Meta One subscription plans and automated WhatsApp Business features. The market is rapidly bifurcating, with search optimization startup Profound reaching a unicorn valuation, while automation platform Relay shut down under pressure from big tech platforms.
