# Anthropic’s new Fable release is cheaper, less restrictive

Anthropic has released Fable 5.1, the latest iteration of its artificial intelligence model. This update specifically targets operational efficiency by introducing structural changes designed to lower overall token costs. In addition to cost savings, the release refines the model's safety systems to decrease the occurrence of false-positive blocks. By tuning these safeguard parameters, the system provides a more seamless and reliable experience for developers and end users. Ultimately, the key takeaways of the Fable 5.1 release are its reduced operating costs and a significantly less restrictive output system.
