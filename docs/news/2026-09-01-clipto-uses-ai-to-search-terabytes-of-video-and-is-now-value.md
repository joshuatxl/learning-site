# Clipto uses AI to search terabytes of video and is now valued at $250M

Three-year-old startup Clipto utilizes artificial intelligence to enable users to efficiently search through terabytes of video data. Demonstrating remarkably swift commercial traction, the company successfully scaled its business to reach $15 million in annual recurring revenue. Furthermore, Clipto managed to achieve profitability before seeking any additional outside capital. To support its continued growth, the company recently secured a fresh $15 million funding round. Ultimately, reaching a $250 million valuation while maintaining profitability demonstrates the immense market demand and strong investor confidence in AI-driven video search technologies.
