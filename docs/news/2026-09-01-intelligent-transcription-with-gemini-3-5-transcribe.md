# Intelligent transcription with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe

Gemini 3.5 Transcribe has officially launched, bringing enhanced intelligence to speech-to-text transcription services. Powered by advanced AI models, this update focuses on improving contextual comprehension and overall accuracy when converting spoken audio into written text. The system is engineered to perform reliably even in complex audio environments featuring background noise or multiple speakers. It provides developers and enterprise users with a more efficient solution for managing both real-time and pre-recorded audio processing workflows. Ultimately, the key takeaways are that Gemini 3.5 Transcribe delivers superior transcription precision, smarter contextual awareness, and boosted efficiency for speech recognition tasks.
