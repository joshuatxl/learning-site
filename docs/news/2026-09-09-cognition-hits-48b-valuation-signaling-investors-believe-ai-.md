# Cognition hits $48B valuation, signaling investors believe AI coding is far from a winner-take-all market

![](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Scott-Wu-of-Cognition.jpg?w=1024)

Cognition has achieved a $48 billion valuation, signaling that investors believe the AI coding industry is far from a winner-take-all market. Additionally, Cognition's valuation multiple is higher than Cursor's was prior to Cursor being sold to SpaceX.
