# Granite 4.2 LLMs: How They're Built

IBM's Granite 4.2 large language models (LLMs) represent a targeted approach to building high-performance, open-source AI tailored for enterprise applications. The development process relies on meticulously curated datasets that focus heavily on programming code, mathematical reasoning, and domain-specific business data. To maximize performance while controlling operational costs, the engineering team utilized efficient Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures paired with IBM's InstructLab alignment methodology. Furthermore, robust data filtering and safety guardrails were embedded directly into the training pipeline to mitigate copyright risks and harmful outputs. **Ultimately, the key takeaways of Granite 4.2 are its open-source accessibility, computational efficiency, and enterprise-first design that combines top-tier performance with strict safety and compliance standards.**
