# Harbor Adapters and Harbor-Index: Infrastructure and a Curated Meta-Dataset for Large-Scale Agentic Evaluation

![](/static/browse/0.3.4/images/arxiv-logo-fb.png)

To simplify agentic evaluation across complex environments, researchers developed Harbor Adapters, an open-source infrastructure that adapts over 80 benchmarks for testing arbitrary AI agents. Using this platform, the authors conducted a large-scale evaluation of eight models across 54 benchmarks and introduced Harbor-Index, a curated and affordable meta-dataset of 82 challenging tasks from 29 benchmarks. Across all evaluated model-harness configurations on Harbor-Index, no model exceeded a 30% pass rate, demonstrating the suite's high difficulty and effectiveness for testing agent capabilities.
