# Harvard Law dropout raises $6M for Blue Voice to build a ‘Harvey for police officers’

Blue Voice, a startup founded by a Harvard Law dropout, has successfully raised $6 million in funding to build an artificial intelligence tool tailored for law enforcement. Described as a "Harvey for police officers," the platform aims to provide specialized digital support during daily police operations. Unlike general-purpose AI systems, Blue Voice relies on localized datasets, including department-specific laws, local ordinances, and internal operational protocols. Because these specialized guidelines contain proprietary information, standard AI tools cannot access them on the public internet. In summary, the key takeaway is that Blue Voice is utilizing its $6 million funding to fill a critical gap in public safety technology by delivering a specialized AI assistant trained on exclusive, non-public law enforcement data.
