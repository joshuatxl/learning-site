# Quantization-Aware Healing: a compressed, 4-bit model that outperforms its full-precision original

A new AI compression technique known as Quantization-Aware Healing (QAH) effectively addresses the traditional trade-off between model size and performance. The process reduces large neural network models to a highly efficient 4-bit architecture, drastically cutting memory usage and computational demands. Unlike standard quantization methods that typically degrade accuracy, QAH applies targeted fine-tuning to actively repair the performance loss introduced during the compression stage. Remarkably, the resulting 4-bit model not only retains its capabilities, but actually surpasses the accuracy and overall performance of its original, full-precision baseline. **The key takeaway is that Quantization-Aware Healing proves AI models can be drastically compressed for faster, cheaper deployment while simultaneously achieving superior performance over their uncompressed predecessors.**
