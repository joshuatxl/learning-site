# Apple shares ‘shocking evidence’ against former employee accused of stealing company data for OpenAI

Apple has uncovered what it describes as "shocking evidence" against a former employee accused of stealing proprietary company data to benefit OpenAI. The tech giant alleges that the worker exfiltrated sensitive trade secrets and confidential information prior to leaving his role. Furthermore, Apple revealed that the individual deliberately attempted to cover his tracks by destroying evidence of the data theft upon learning he was under investigation. This alleged obstruction has significantly escalated the legal dispute, shedding light on the aggressive measures companies are taking to protect their internal developments. Ultimately, the key takeaways from this case highlight the escalating battle for AI dominance, the growing threat of insider intellectual property theft, and the severe legal consequences of attempting to destroy digital evidence.
