# The Open ASR Leaderboard Adds Its First Global South Language

Hugging Face's Open ASR Leaderboard has expanded its evaluation framework by incorporating its first language from the Global South. Traditionally, speech recognition benchmarks have focused heavily on high-resource Western languages, leading to biased performance metrics and neglected low-resource dialects. The inclusion of this new language enables researchers to rigorously test and compare automatic speech recognition models against underrepresented speech datasets. This initiative reflects a growing push within the tech community to address regional disparities in artificial intelligence training data. **Key Takeaway:** Ultimately, integrating Global South languages into standardized AI benchmarks fosters greater technological inclusivity, driving the development of speech recognition tools that accurately serve historically underrepresented populations worldwide.
