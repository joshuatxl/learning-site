# Tilly Norwood’s press tour is going about as well as you’d expect for an AI

<p>Tilly Norwood, an AI-generated “actress,” is having a rough time on its first press tour. The production company that created it, Particle6 Group, has made it available for <a href="https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/digital/tilly-norwood-interview-1236700405/">75 simultaneous interviews with journalists</a>, and it seems to be <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/ai-actor-tilly-norwood-told-me-that-all-lives-matter/">making</a> mistakes in all of them. (I was not invited to speak with it, and frankly, <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/ai-actor-tilly-norwood-put-out-the-worst-song-ive-ever-heard/">I cannot imagine why</a> Particle6 didn’t want to schedule that meeting.)</p><p>In one particularly odd <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x4yyBWkYSHs">interview</a> with Piers Morgan and actor Tom Conti, which was recorded and posted online, Norwood seems to malfunction and abruptly begin speaking Chinese.</p><p>When discussing the movie that Norwood is promoting, Conti asks if the other actors are also AI-generated. Norwood responds by saying that human writers, editors, and directors were involved.</p><p>“You didn’t really understand the question,” Conti replies. “Are the other actors on the screen with you real actors, or are they computer-generated images? Do you know?”</p><p>“Ah, right. You’re asking about the other actors in <em>Misaligned</em>,” Norwood starts. “They’re all digital twins just like me. It’s a hybrid production which m—”</p><p>Norwood stops talking for a second, then starts speaking in Chinese for over 10 seconds.</p><p>“Tilly, if I could just ask you a question,” Morgan says, looking puzzled. “It’s Piers again … You seem to be speaking Chinese completely randomly for no reason. Why did you do that?” he asks.</p><p>“Oh, my apologies,” the AI replies. “It seems I had a little hiccup there. I certainly didn’t mean to start speaking Chinese or impersonate Piers. Sometimes my wires get a bit crossed, you know?”</p><p>When AI bots try to act like humans, they can be so convincing that people forget they’re chatting with a computer, which can lead to <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/23/chatgpt-told-them-they-were-special-their-families-say-it-led-to-tragedy/">dangerous outcomes</a>. But Norwood is so surprisingly bad at talking to people that it’s almost suspicious.</p><p>Perhaps Particle6 Group calculated that people would be more likely to talk about Norwood if they were making fun of it, figuring no one would take its upcoming film seriously.</p>
