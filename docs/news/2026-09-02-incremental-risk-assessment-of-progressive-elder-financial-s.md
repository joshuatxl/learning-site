# Incremental Risk Assessment of Progressive Elder Financial Scams via Instruction-Tuned Small Language Models

Financial scams targeting older adults typically escalate across multiple conversational turns, making early detection difficult without continuous monitoring. To combat this, researchers proposed a cumulative turn-based risk assessment framework that continuously re-evaluates scam risks at every stage of an evolving dialogue. The researchers tested this framework using a newly annotated multi-turn scam dataset to fine-tune four instruction-tuned small language models: Phi-4, LLaMA-3.2, DeepSeek-R1, and Qwen3. Among these, Phi-4 and LLaMA-3.2 performed exceptionally well at recognizing fraud cues and cross-turn escalation patterns relative to their parameter size. Ultimately, the key takeaway is that combining structured cumulative modeling with compact language models offers a powerful, privacy-aware solution for real-time, on-device fraud protection on resource-constrained mobile devices.
