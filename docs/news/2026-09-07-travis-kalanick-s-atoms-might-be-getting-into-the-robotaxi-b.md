# Travis Kalanick’s Atoms might be getting into the robotaxi business

![](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/travis-kalanick-fii.jpg?resize=1200,800)

Uber founder Travis Kalanick appears to be positioning his latest venture, Atoms, to enter the robotaxi industry. Kalanick stated that this move will allow him to address "unfinished business," hinting at a return to the autonomous vehicle ambitions he originally pursued during his time at Uber.
