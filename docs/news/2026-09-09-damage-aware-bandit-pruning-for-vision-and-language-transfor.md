# Damage-Aware Bandit Pruning for Vision and Language Transformers

![](/static/browse/0.3.4/images/arxiv-logo-fb.png)

This paper introduces a damage-aware multi-armed bandit framework to improve structured post-training pruning for vision and language transformers. By evaluating "paired damage" (masked loss minus base loss) on calibration batches, the method sequentially identifies and zeroes out attention heads and MLP channels with minimal performance loss. Experiments across a wide range of models demonstrate that this approach consistently reduces performance degradation compared to traditional pruning baselines under a fixed evaluation budget.
