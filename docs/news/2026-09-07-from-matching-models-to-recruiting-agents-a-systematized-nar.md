# From Matching Models to Recruiting Agents: A Systematized Narrative Review of AI Recruitment Systems, Evaluation, and Governance

![](/static/browse/0.3.4/images/arxiv-logo-fb.png)

This systematized narrative review traces the evolution of AI recruitment systems from basic profile-matching models to multi-stage workflows and tool-using recruiting agents. Based on an analysis of 40 representative works, the study highlights critical gaps, such as reliance on private data, hidden pipeline failures, and a complete lack of joint evaluations covering utility, fairness, privacy, and security. To address these limitations, the authors introduce a staged evaluation framework and an agenda for auditable, evidence-grounded recruitment systems governed by explicit cost and risk constraints.
