# Google’s answer to Canva is an AI tool where you prompt instead of design

Google is expanding its footprint in the creative software market with the introduction of a new tool called Google Pics. The platform aims to compete directly with industry giants like Canva and Adobe. Rather than relying on traditional visual editing tools, Google Pics adopts a completely AI-first methodology. Users can create visual content simply by entering text prompts instead of manually building designs. Ultimately, the key takeaways are that Google Pics introduces an AI-first paradigm to visual creation by prioritizing text prompting over manual design to challenge established market leaders.
