# Training and Finetuning Multi-Vector Embedding Models with Sentence Transformers

Sentence Transformers has expanded its capabilities to natively support the training and fine-tuning of multi-vector embedding models like ColBERT. Unlike conventional single-vector methods that condense an entire document into one dense vector, multi-vector architectures generate distinct embeddings for individual tokens to capture fine-grained semantic context. The updated framework provides streamlined pipelines for custom loss functions, data preparation, and evaluation, simplifying what was previously a complex process. Consequently, developers can now build and optimize domain-specific retrieval systems with far greater search precision. Ultimately, this update democratizes state-of-the-art retrieval techniques by making highly accurate multi-vector models as accessible and easy to train as traditional dense embeddings.
