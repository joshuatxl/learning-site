# New experts join Google’s AI & Economy team

<h2>New experts join Google’s AI &amp; Economy team</h2><p>Nobel Laureate Philippe Aghion, Professor Ajay Agrawal, and leading researchers join Google’s AI &amp; Economy program to expand our scientific understanding of AI’s impact on economic activity worldwide.</p><p>Head of StratOps and Special Projects, Technology &amp; Society</p><p>AI &amp; Economy Lead, Chief Economist&#x27;s Office</p><p>Google is expanding its AI &amp; Economy Research Program by adding world-class economists and academic advisors to better track how artificial intelligence impacts jobs and productivity. New directors Anu Madgavkar and Daniel Rock will lead this effort, focusing on global AI adoption, labor market shifts, and enterprise growth. You can explore their latest data and research publications at ai.google/economy to see how these insights shape future policy and workforce training.</p><ul><li>Google’s &quot;New experts join Google’s AI &amp; Economy team&quot; article details their expanded research program.</li><li>Google is hiring world-class economists to study how AI changes jobs and the economy.</li><li>New directors and academic advisors will analyze AI’s impact on productivity and global growth.</li><li>This team will use real-time data to help workers and policymakers navigate AI shifts.</li><li>You can explore their latest research and data at ai.google/economy to learn more.</li></ul><p>Google is hiring top economists to study how AI changes our jobs and the economy. These experts will track how people use AI tools to help businesses and workers adapt to the future. By analyzing this data, they hope to make sure AI helps everyone succeed. You can check out their latest research and findings on their website.</p><h3>Explore other styles:</h3><p>We recently launched the <a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/understanding-the-ai-economy/">AI &amp; Economy ATLAS</a> v1.0 and its <a href="https://ai.google/economy/atlas/">interactive open-access site</a> to understand how people are using Google’s AI tools at work and in their daily lives. But tracking adoption patterns is only the beginning. As artificial intelligence reshapes jobs, businesses, and everyday lives, navigating this shift requires a multidisciplinary approach that unites fine-grained data with rigorous economic inquiry.</p><p>To further help organizations, workers, researchers, and policymakers make sense of this complex transition, we are expanding our <a href="https://ai.google/economy/">AI &amp; Economy Research Program</a>, and welcoming world-class economists to help build and lead this work.</p><p>Technological shifts are rarely instantaneous. Our program measures and analyzes this evolution in real time, focusing on core areas including the future of work, productivity and growth, global technology diffusion, and AI’s impact on scientific discovery.</p><h2>Expanding our scientific expertise and engagement</h2><p>Executing a research agenda of this scope requires deep collaboration among academia, industry leaders, and policymakers. To expand our scientific capacity and engage more deeply with these diverse stakeholders, we are bringing together leading external advisors, visiting scholars, and dedicated research program leadership. Joining us as Academic Advisors and Visiting Fellows:</p><ul><li><strong>Philippe Aghion, Academic Advisor:</strong> Professor Aghion, 2025 Nobel Laureate in Economics and the Kurt Björklund Chaired Professor at INSEAD and the Collège de France, joins our advisory group alongside Nobel Laureate Michael Spence and Cambridge’s Dame Diane Coyle, applying his pioneering work on innovation-led growth and creative destruction to model AI&#x27;s long-term macroeconomic trajectory.</li><li><strong>Ajay Agrawal, Visiting Fellow:</strong> Professor Agrawal joins our Visiting Fellows program, where he will collaborate closely with David Autor, a current Fellow and head of MIT’s Department of Economics. Professor Agrawal, who holds the Geoffrey Taber Chair in Entrepreneurship and Innovation and is a Professor of Strategic Management at the University of Toronto’s Rotman School of Management, will advance our core research on the economics of AI and scientific discovery, AI and robotics, and how AI can expand the frontier of human welfare.</li></ul><p>To steer empirical projects and integrate insights across our agenda, we are also introducing two renowned researchers as Directors of Google’s AI &amp; Economy Research Program:</p><ul><li><strong>Anu Madgavkar:</strong> Formerly a Partner at the McKinsey Global Institute (MGI), Madgavkar joins Google following a two-decade career leading global research on labor markets, technology adoption, and structural economic transitions. She has frequently advised governments, multilateral institutions, and business leaders worldwide, contributing regularly to the World Economic Forum and the United Nations. At Google, she will lead empirical research on global AI diffusion, small business ecosystems, and the workforce impacts of generative AI.</li><li><strong>Daniel Rock:</strong> Joining from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, Professor Rock is a leading economist on AI and labor, having co-authored foundational papers on technological transitions. An AI2050 Early Career Fellow and MIT Digital Fellow, he will lead Google&#x27;s empirical research, bridging frontier model telemetry with rigorous econometrics to analyze enterprise productivity, labor restructuring, and scientific discovery.</li></ul><p>Anu Madgavkar and Daniel Rock will lead Google&#x27;s AI &amp; Economy Research Program alongside Alex Imas, Director of AGI Economics at Google DeepMind, and Zanna Iscenko, AI &amp; Economy Lead, in Google&#x27;s Chief Economist&#x27;s Office.</p><h2>Shaping future research and collective impact</h2><p>Maximizing AI’s economic opportunity while mitigating disruption requires sustained partnership. This expanded team of experts will serve as a scientific bridge, directly informing future ATLAS updates and empirical research.</p><p>Together, we will focus on identifying the organizational practices, public policy frameworks, and training programs needed to ensure AI upskills workers, democratizes expertise, and drives broadly shared prosperity.</p><p>To learn more, read our latest research publications, and interact with our latest global data, visit <a href="https://ai.google/economy/">ai.google/economy</a>.</p>
