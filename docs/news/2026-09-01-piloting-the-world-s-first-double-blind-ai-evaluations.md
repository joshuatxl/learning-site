# Piloting the world's first double-blind AI evaluations

A novel initiative has introduced the world's first double-blind evaluation process for advanced artificial intelligence models. In this pilot program, human evaluators assess model outputs without knowing which specific developer or AI system generated them. Drawing inspiration from medical clinical trials, this approach aims to remove brand bias and subjective expectations from safety and performance testing. As frontier AI capabilities accelerate, establishing standardized and objective testing environments has become critical for global tech governance. Overall, this double-blind pilot significantly reduces evaluator bias, elevates scientific rigor in AI safety standards, and provides a trustworthy framework for future regulatory oversight.
