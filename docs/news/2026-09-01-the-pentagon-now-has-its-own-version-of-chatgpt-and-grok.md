# The Pentagon now has its own version of ChatGPT and Grok

The United States Pentagon has integrated customized versions of OpenAI's ChatGPT and Grok into its official technology infrastructure. These newly added platforms will join Google's Gemini on the defense department's central portal dedicated to artificial intelligence tools. By hosting multiple leading models, the portal provides defense personnel with tailored access to top-tier generative AI systems within secure environments. This move underscores an accelerating effort by military leadership to harness private-sector AI innovation for government applications. Ultimately, the key takeaway is that the Pentagon is actively diversifying its AI portfolio across major tech vendors to drive operational efficiency and strengthen national security capabilities.
