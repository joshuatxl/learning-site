# Authors push back as publishers and agents make claims on Anthropic settlement

![](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/49688202642_09fd5ccc38_k.jpg?resize=1200,800)

Authors are disputing the distribution of funds from a recent copyright settlement with the AI company Anthropic. They contend that publishers and literary agents are attempting to claim an unfairly large portion of the payout. As a result, creators are pushing back to ensure they receive proper compensation for the use of their work.
