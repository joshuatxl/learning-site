# Inside our 353,000-person vibe coding course

<h2>Inside our 353,000-person vibe coding course</h2><p>Google and Kaggle’s “AI Agents: Intensive Vibe Coding” course had expert-led sessions, technical whitepapers and notebooks, and capstone projects.</p><p>Group AI Product Manager, Founder of GenAI intensive</p><p>Partnerships Lead, Kaggle</p><p>Over 353,000 developers recently joined our five-day intensive course to master building and deploying AI agents using natural language. Participants collaborated on Discord to debug code and submitted over 6,000 impressive capstone projects, ranging from historical transcription tools to space-weather research systems. You can still access all course materials on the Kaggle Learn website to sharpen your skills at your own pace.</p><ul><li>&quot;Inside our 353,000-person vibe coding course&quot; explores how we teach modern AI development.</li><li>Over 353,000 people joined our intensive course to learn about building AI agents.</li><li>Participants used natural language to program and move prototypes from vibe to live.</li><li>A massive community collaborated on Discord to debug code and share new ideas.</li><li>You can still access all course materials online to learn at your own pace.</li></ul><p>Google and Kaggle hosted a massive online course to teach people how to build AI agents using simple language. Over 350,000 people joined to learn how to turn their ideas into real, working projects. Participants collaborated on Discord to solve problems and create impressive tools together. You can still check out the lessons online if you want to learn these skills yourself.</p><h3>Explore other styles:</h3><p>AI is moving faster than traditional methods of learning and teaching can keep up with it. With new concepts, skills, ideas, and tools arriving almost daily, we needed a new way to teach and learn these new technologies.</p><p>Since Google and Kaggle’s first “5 Day Intensive” course in 2024 more than 2 million learners and developers have participated in the multimodal, no cost courses. Our latest collaboration, the 5-Day AI Agents: Intensive Vibe Coding Course with Google, had over 353,000 registered participants. Together, we explored the frontier of vibe coding, where people program through natural language. Our courses navigated the entire lifecycle of designing, securing, and deploying production-grade AI agents in the cloud. The response proved that developers are ready to graduate their prototypes &quot;from vibe to live”.</p><p>Hundreds of thousands of learners gathered across codelabs and technical whitepapers to collaborate in real time. The heart of this collaboration was on <a href="https://discord.com/invite/kaggle">Kaggle&#x27;s Discord</a>, where over 392,000 active participants swapped code, debugged together, and formed study groups.</p><p>The capstone projects brought the week’s learning into action. We received over 6,000 project submissions from more than 12,000 active capstone participants.</p><p>The projects demonstrated the immense creativity, technical skill, and imagination of the global AI developer community. From advanced digital humanities tools like the historical manuscript transcription pipeline <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/vibecoding-agents-capstone-project/writeups/palimpsest">Palimpsest</a>, to sophisticated space-weather research systems like<a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/vibecoding-agents-capstone-project/writeups/project-aries"> Project ARIES</a>, the caliber of submissions made it incredibly difficult to narrow down the top entries. You can explore all the winning teams and their <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/vibecoding-agents-capstone-project/hackathon-winners">capstone projects</a> on our website.</p><p>We look forward to continuing our partnership and exploring future learning opportunities. If you’re interested in learning more, you can continue honing your skills through ongoing <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions">Kaggle competitions</a> and stay connected with fellow learners on the <a href="https://discord.com/invite/kaggle">Kaggle Discord</a>. For those who missed the live event in June but still want to learn, all course content remains available as a self-paced <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/learn-guide/5-day-agents-vibecoding">Kaggle Learn guide</a>.</p>
