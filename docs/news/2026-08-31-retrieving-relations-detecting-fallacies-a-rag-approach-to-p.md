# Retrieving Relations, Detecting Fallacies: A RAG Approach to Political Debate Analysis

Detecting fallacious arguments in political debates is crucial for public discourse, yet automatic identification requires complex contextual and relational knowledge beyond simple text analysis. To overcome the limitations of traditional static classifiers, researchers developed a novel framework that dynamically retrieves relevant context using argumentative relations such as support and attack. The methodology was evaluated on the ElecDeb60to20 benchmark across 14 distinct models, leveraging a 15GB political knowledge base. Experimental results showed substantial performance improvements over non-retrieval baselines, reaching macro-F1 scores of up to 0.864 for fallacy detection and 0.725 for classification. Ultimately, the key takeaway is that dynamically incorporating external knowledge through argumentatively guided retrieval significantly enhances an AI system's ability to accurately identify and categorize logical fallacies in political discourse.
