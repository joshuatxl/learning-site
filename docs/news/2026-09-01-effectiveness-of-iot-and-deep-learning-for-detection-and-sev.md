# Effectiveness of IoT and Deep Learning for Detection and Severity Assessment of Postelectrotermes militaris in Tea Plantations

Researchers have introduced an innovative IoT and deep-learning framework designed to detect and assess Upcountry Live Wood Termite infestations in tea plantations. The non-invasive system utilizes Raspberry Pi-connected microphones to capture tree trunk audio, transforming the sound data into spectrograms for analysis. A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) classifies whether a tree is infested, while a secondary model evaluates infestation severity by incorporating audio amplitude and geospatial proximity to nearby affected plants. During field trials conducted under realistic environmental noise conditions, the AI model demonstrated solid performance, achieving an 81.5% accuracy rate on the test data. The key takeaway is that this technology effectively combines acoustic AI detection with geospatial mapping, empowering plantation managers to identify high-risk areas early, prioritize field inspections, and implement targeted pest control measures.
