# AfterQuery reportedly becomes Y Combinator’s fastest-ever unicorn, now valued at $3.2B

AI model-training startup AfterQuery has reportedly achieved a major milestone by becoming Y Combinator’s fastest-ever unicorn. According to recent reports, the company reached a new valuation of $3.2 billion following its latest funding round. This remarkable growth occurred over a span of just five months, following a $30 million Series A round completed in April. At the time of that earlier round, the startup was valued at just $300 million. Ultimately, the key takeaways are that AfterQuery achieved a record-breaking tenfold valuation surge in less than half a year, illustrating the immense investor demand and rapid growth currently surrounding AI infrastructure startups.
