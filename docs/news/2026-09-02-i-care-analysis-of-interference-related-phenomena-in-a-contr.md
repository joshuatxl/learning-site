# I-CARE: Analysis of interference-related phenomena in a controllable, diverse and representative unlearning setting for text-to-image models

Machine unlearning aims to remove specific knowledge from AI models, but it often leads to "interference," where unintended degradation occurs to semantically related concepts that should be retained. To address the poor characterization and inconsistent evaluation of this problem in text-to-image models, researchers introduced a new methodology called I-CARE. Instead of introducing a transient benchmark, I-CARE establishes formal definitions, standardized metrics, and reporting templates designed to adapt as unlearning algorithms evolve. The practical viability of the framework was successfully demonstrated using state-of-the-art algorithms, and the system was released alongside an intuitive, web-based interface. Ultimately, the key takeaways are that I-CARE establishes a standardized, future-proof approach for systematically studying interference in machine unlearning while offering open-source tools that make these insights easily accessible to researchers.
