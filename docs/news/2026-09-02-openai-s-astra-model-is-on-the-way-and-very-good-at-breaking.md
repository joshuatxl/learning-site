# OpenAI’s Astra model is on the way — and very good at breaking into computer systems

OpenAI is preparing for the release of its newest large language model, named Astra. Recent testing indicates that the model possesses advanced capabilities in exploiting software vulnerabilities and breaking into computer systems. Because of these capabilities, OpenAI has categorized Astra as a cyber-critical AI that poses unique digital security risks. To address these concerns, the company has previewed a series of safety precautions, testing frameworks, and guardrails designed to prevent malicious misuse. Ultimately, the key takeaway is that Astra's potent cyber-offensive abilities highlight a growing shift where AI safety and proactive threat containment must be prioritized alongside technical advancement.
