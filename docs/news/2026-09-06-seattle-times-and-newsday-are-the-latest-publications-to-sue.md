# Seattle Times and Newsday are the latest publications to sue OpenAI and Microsoft

The Seattle Times and Newsday have joined a growing list of news organizations suing OpenAI and Microsoft. The publications allege that the tech companies used their journalistic content without authorization to train artificial intelligence models.
