# From Atari to EVE Online: Building on 15 Years of AI Research in Games

For over fifteen years, Google DeepMind has used video games as a primary benchmark for artificial intelligence, progressing from mastering retro Atari classics to navigating complex, player-driven worlds like *EVE Online*. This historical evolution enabled researchers to advance machine learning from simple reflex-based actions toward sophisticated long-term strategy and multi-agent cooperation. Moving beyond pure research, DeepMind is now actively partnering with game studios to bring these advanced models directly into game production. Through these collaborations, developers and researchers are prototyping groundbreaking gameplay features, including highly adaptive non-player characters and dynamic, evolving environments. **Ultimately, the key takeaway is that fifteen years of foundational AI research in games is now successfully transitioning into practical development, promising to revolutionize interactive game design and create richer player experiences.**
