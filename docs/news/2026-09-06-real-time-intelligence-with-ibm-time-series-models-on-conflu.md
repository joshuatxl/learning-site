# Real-Time Intelligence with IBM Time Series Models on Confluent

IBM has integrated its AI-driven time series models with Confluent's data streaming platform to enable real-time predictive analytics. This collaboration allows businesses to continuously analyze live sequential data streams to generate immediate, actionable intelligence. By unifying real-time data infrastructure with advanced forecasting, organizations can significantly improve automated decision-making for operations, demand planning, and risk management.
