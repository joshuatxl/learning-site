# Fine-tuning a 350M Model for Better Structured Outputs in 100 GRPO Steps

This article highlights a method for fine-tuning a compact 350-million parameter AI model to significantly improve its ability to generate reliable, structured outputs. By utilizing Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), the model achieved enhanced performance in remarkably few training iterations—just 100 steps. This demonstrates a highly efficient, low-resource approach for optimizing smaller models for specialized formatting tasks.
