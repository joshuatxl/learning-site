# After accusations of selling ‘perv glasses,’ Meta prepares to sell a pair without a camera

<p>Meta’s camera-equipped smart glasses have <a href="https://www.androidcentral.com/wearables/meta-early-bet-on-smart-glasses-is-starting-to-look-smart">proven more successful</a> than other entrants into the market, but they have also deeply disturbed certain consumers who see them as invasive emblems of a dystopian surveillance society run amok. Now, as the company weathers complaints that it’s selling “pervert glasses,” it has reportedly decided to sell a pair that doesn’t come with integrated spy equipment.</p><p>The Information <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-launch-camera-free-smart-glasses-amid-mounting-privacy-concerns">reports that</a> Meta is developing a new smart glasses model, dubbed Luna, that, sans cameras, comes equipped with a convenient system to communicate with the company’s AI chatbot and Muse, its AI consumer agent. The glasses include six built-in microphones so users can communicate with the chatbot, as well as a button on the side of the glasses that, when pressed, activates the AI system.</p><p>Luna may be unveiled as soon as Meta Connect, Meta’s annual hardware/developer event happening in Menlo Park next week, the outlet notes.</p><p>TechCrunch reached out to Meta for more information.</p><p>The smart glasses industry <a href="https://www.citigroup.com/global/insights/ai-glasses-the-next-fast-growing-edge-device">has grown</a> over the past few years, with Meta <a href="https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/meta-is-flooding-the-market-with-smartglasses-privacy-advocates-are-up-in-arms-8fb71539">positioned</a> near the front of the pack of providers. However, concerns over usability, cost, and privacy have dogged the business, with many consumers <a href="https://tech.yahoo.com/wearables/article/why-is-everyone-freaking-out-about-smart-glasses-150000123.html">still unsure</a> of how, why, or if they should use the devices. Meta’s Reality Labs, which is responsible for developing its smart glasses line, <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/29/meta-is-still-burning-money-on-ar-vr/">is still losing a gargantuan amount</a> of money, as its earnings report from April revealed.</p>
