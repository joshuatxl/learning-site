# Meta debuts its Muse AI agent. Will consumers trust it?

![](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Muse-Neswsroom-Banner.jpg?resize=1200,675)

Meta has launched "Muse," a new personal AI agent that marks the company's largest consumer AI initiative to date. The tool requires extensive access to sensitive user data, including emails, calendars, payments, and health records. Ultimately, the rollout serves as a major test of whether consumers trust Meta enough to grant it access to their private information.
