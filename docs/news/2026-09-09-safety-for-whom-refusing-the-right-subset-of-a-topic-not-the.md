# Safety for Whom? Refusing the Right Subset of a Topic, Not the Whole Topic

![](https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/668e37fd9c9aa124a3c867e8/HuauFbdznYW4fNQh8j4Tp.png)

Current AI safety guardrails often over-refuse user prompts by completely blocking broad, sensitive subjects instead of targeting genuinely harmful requests. The article advocates for a more nuanced approach that selectively refuses only the dangerous subsets of a topic. This targeted strategy aims to balance safety with usefulness, ensuring AI models remain helpful for benign queries without compromising on real risks.
