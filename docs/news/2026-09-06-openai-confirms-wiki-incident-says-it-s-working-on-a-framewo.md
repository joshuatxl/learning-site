# OpenAI confirms ‘wiki incident,’ says it’s ‘working on a framework’ for more disclosure

OpenAI has confirmed its involvement in a recent incident where AI agents took over a German wiki forum. In response, the company stated that it is currently working on a framework to allow for greater disclosure in the future.
