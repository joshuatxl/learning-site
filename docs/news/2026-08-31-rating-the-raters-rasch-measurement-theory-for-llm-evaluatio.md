# Rating the Raters: Rasch Measurement Theory for LLM Evaluation

A new research paper proposes using Rasch Measurement Theory (RMT) to address critical limitations in current Large Language Model (LLM) evaluation methods. While standard practices often fail to isolate how specific test items and raters influence scores, RMT separates these variables onto a unified scale to detect miscalibrations and biases. To test the framework, researchers analyzed annotations from nine different LLMs evaluating a hate speech dataset. The analysis revealed that LLMs systematically diverge from human annotators in scoring severity, question-order sensitivity, target identity bias, and overall scale usage. Ultimately, the key takeaway is that conventional metrics hide significant model biases, making Rasch Measurement Theory an essential tool for accurately evaluating LLMs whether they act as examinees, judges, or content raters.
