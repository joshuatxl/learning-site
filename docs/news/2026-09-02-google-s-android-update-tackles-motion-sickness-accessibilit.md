# Google’s Android update tackles motion sickness, accessibility, and more

Google has announced a new Android update designed to introduce a variety of system enhancements and user features. Among these additions are new tools aimed at tackling motion sickness while traveling, alongside general improvements to overall device accessibility. Certain capabilities bring Android in line with Apple, matching features that iPhone users have already been utilizing. Additionally, Google is directly leveraging its Gemini AI model to power several distinct, next-generation software upgrades across the platform. Overall, the key takeaways of this update are that Google is successfully closing the feature gap with Apple on essential usability tools while simultaneously utilizing its proprietary Gemini AI to drive unique platform innovations.
