AI agents add another option.</p><p>Engineering tasks, customer support, and operational work can increasingly be delegated to systems capable of completing multistep tasks rather than simply helping an employee complete them. That gives founders an opportunity to rethink what their earliest teams should actually look like.</p><p>Instead of automatically asking, “Who do we hire next?,” the starting question can become: “What work needs to be done — and is a person the best way to do it?” That distinction matters.</p><p>Giving work to an AI agent can help a small team move faster, but it also introduces new questions around ownership.
