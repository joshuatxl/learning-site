# Proactive cyber defense for governments and enterprises

Governments and enterprises are increasingly adopting AI-driven proactive cyber defense strategies to shift from reactive incident response to predictive risk mitigation. By leveraging machine learning and real-time threat intelligence, these organizations can identify system vulnerabilities and neutralize cyberattacks before they compromise critical infrastructure. This automated approach ensures continuous, resilient protection against an evolving landscape of sophisticated digital threats.
